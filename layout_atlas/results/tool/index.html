The EC extension only gives layout hints; it never replaces the reactions or stoichiometry of the target GEM.

Maps are drawn with Bézier connections from curated community-map templates. The basic drawing rules are always on: all reactions and metabolites are kept, curated structures and curves are reused, substrates and products are placed on opposite sides, metabolite instances are decided automatically, and reactions are placed beside their own metabolites.
